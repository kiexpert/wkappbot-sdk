Subject: Build contest submission -- Will Kim (wkappbot-sdk)

Hi there,

For the build contest, I am submitting something I already built and use daily, rather than a fresh contest-day project: an AI knowledge-compounding harness, live at kiexpert.github.io/wkappbot-sdk/skills/.

The idea: AI agents are amnesiac between sessions -- they hit the same wall twice. My harness turns key lessons into mechanical guards instead of documentation a model can ignore, so the next session inherits what the last one learned the hard way. Point a cheap model at your team own campaign playbooks and past creative decisions through a system like this, and it can start running your best practices reliably -- the accumulated knowledge does the heavy lifting, not a bigger model.

Tonight is a live example: I hit a browser-automation bug, recorded the fix as a skill, and the very next attempt succeeded in under 5 minutes instead of hours.

This is daily real use, not a mockup: 400+ dated skills from real work (browser/desktop automation, legal case tracking, job-search automation, trading bots), publicly browsable now with a privacy filter designed to keep personal repos out.

Happy to walk through it live.
