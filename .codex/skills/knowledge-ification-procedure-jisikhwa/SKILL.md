# Knowledge-ification procedure jisikhwa

## Steps

1. TRIGGER: when the user says knowledge-ify (Korean jisikhwa) or a session completes notable work, the repo AI runs this procedure BY DEFAULT, no extra prompting needed.@@SCOPE: capture the AI OWN recent experience plus the process just performed: decisions made, gotchas hit, commands that worked, failures and their fixes, and the why behind each.@@3-TIER SKILLIFY: split into T1 core (decision rules, when-to-use), T2 howto (step-by-step commands and procedure), T3 ref (dated history, lessons, incidents). Cross-link with next and cluster refs. English-only content per encoding guard.@@PER-REPO INTELLIGENCE: each repo agent does this for its own domain; the resulting knowledge lives in that repo knowledge tree and is enforced by the harness so future agents must consult it.@@PURPOSE: the official procedure for the intelligence-ification of knowledge -- lived experience becomes structured, tiered, cross-linked, enforced, reusable knowledge. This grows the Living Knowledge Tree. See ki-concept-definitions and jensen-gtc-harness-agent-vs-wkharness.
